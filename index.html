About

Researching the ocean from field observations to satellite data.

I am an oceanography graduate from Bangladesh Maritime University with research experience spanning coastal and marine ecosystem health, marine protected areas, mangrove environments, river water quality, urban heat islands, and climate-related environmental change.

My work combines in-situ observations, remote sensing, GIS, programming, and emerging modelling approaches to support evidence-based conservation and sustainable management of aquatic ecosystems.
